A field guide to a fictional chorus

The Zampanio Voice Bible How to write each voice in the corpus — baseline, break, tic, and real lines to imitate.

// what this is & who made it

This document was written by Claude, an AI language model made by Anthropic. It is not authored by JR (the creator of Zampanio) or by any of the characters described in it. It is one model's analysis, commissioned by JR as a tool — treat it as a careful hypothesis, not as authoritative fact about the work.

How it was made: JR fed the actual Zampanio source files — game code, in-character documents, chat-log fiction, and design notes — into a conversation with Claude. Claude read those files programmatically, counted punctuation and word patterns across each character, and grouped the voices by a single observed pattern (described below). Every "sample line" is a real quote pulled directly from JR's source files; the descriptions and groupings are Claude's inferences from that text.

Known limits: Zampanio is a collaborative project — some voices were written by JR and some by collaborators, and during analysis Claude could not always tell the two apart. Authorship labels have intentionally been left off this version pending confirmation. A character's "sample lines" are trustworthy; the claim that a given voice is characteristically JR's is not, unless JR has confirmed it. Where a model describes a human's own creative voice, the model is the least reliable witness in the room.

The one rule under everything

Every voice = a baseline register + what a break in it means. To write any of them: set those two, then add the single tic. The whole cast sorts by how it behaves under pressure — does it tighten, shatter into caps, collapse into lowercase, or perform cheer over dread?

00

The author's own registers

The modes the writer works in — the raw material the characters are built from. (These describe an authorial hand; see the provenance note before treating any as a fingerprint.)

Code-confessional— comments left in the source

Lowercase and fast, with feeling smuggled into notes that are supposed to be technical. The leak is the whole point.

Baseline: lowercase dev-comment. Break: it doesn't break — emotion just bleeds in where logic should be.

Tic: :( as a coding emotion; terse-terse-terse then one runaway line.

sample lines

// every time i do subclasses in javascript i regret it

// please stop the shopping spam :( :( :(

// its so. so. scary

// death is real / my rules / i want to imagine they die

// welp, at least only YOU freeze to death, not the whole world

Braindump— raw notes

Pre-articulation. No sentence boundaries; ideas chained by adjacency.

Baseline: unpunctuated stream. Break: n/a — nothing is shaped yet.

Tic: timestamps and stray fragments left in mid-thought.

sample lines

need more reasons for people to hang around with ghosts blackbirds cipher wasted ominous song

one password is aviary full of animated gull skeletons * another is just skelejr sitting in her wheelchair

Sincere— the "JustTruth" register

The masks off entirely. Warm, direct, and — notably — composed, not leaky. Sincerity here is built, not spilled.

Baseline: calm lowercase prose. Break: stays steady; no horror affect.

Tic: earnest statements of motive; the closing *Thank you* for playing my game.

sample lines

what I want more than anything is to be surprised

I really enjoy collaborating! I want to see what unexpected connections other people make

A new friend had the idea of 'maybe zampanio is on Gopher' and so now I'm gonna learn Gopher!

01

The LitRPGSim ensemble

The chat-log saga and its principals — the densest, most varied set of voices in the work.

Wodin/ odinsRazor — the manic obsessive

ALL CAPS, always; the caps are the character. Drops to lowercase only when something real slips through.

Baseline: all-caps, on at all hours. Break: a lowercase word = a true feeling escaping the mania.

Tic: escalating one-line bursts; L337 SKILLZ; link-dumps mid-thought; refuses to sleep.

sample lines

THIS IS THE MOST CONFUSED CREATURE OF ALL TIME

YOU THINK THE POLICE HAVE MY L337 SKILLZ? I ALREADY CRACKED THEIR DATABASE

I KNEW I RECOGNIZED IT / I KNEW I DID

BUT.... (here the caps fail — the tell)

Todd/ theBestDude72 — the grounding friend

The structural mirror of Wodin: lowercase, one clause per line, a period on everything. Breaks down into grief, never up into mania.

Baseline: lowercase, line-broken, period-terminated. Break: restraint tightens; the content does the breaking, not the form.

Tic: a single thought split across many short messages; dry deflation.

sample lines

its.
is screaming 'kill me' directly into my brain.
it is like a little homoculuous covered in pain.

sure.
and the Zodiac Killer likes Mario.

i'm not.
i'm not dumb.
i understand what the police told me. (same form, now grief)

The Closer— corporate menace, total control

The most grammatically controlled voice in the corpus. Balanced, subordinate-clause prose that never breaks. The threat lives entirely under the courtesy.

Baseline: impeccable formal register. Break: never — that is the fingerprint.

Tic: courtesy as a weapon; velvet threats; repeated use of your name.

sample lines

"Hello, Wodin. I'm sure you are alarmed that I am contacting you like this; very sudden, I know. However, please, do not worry."

"I'm afraid that if you publish these accusations without evidence, our lawyers might be inclined to sue for libel. I know it sounds like a threat, but I'd like to assure you that it's not."

"Pathetic. The nerve of some…" (clipped, to her CEO)

Wanda/ CEBro — the gleeful omniscient CEO

All-caps like Wodin, but delighted rather than anxious — and she never gets vulnerable, because she can see the future.

Baseline: all-caps, confident. Break: never breaks to lowercase; no soft register exists.

Tic: BRO, LOL, and DON'T WORRY ABOUT IT as a sinister-kindness refrain.

sample lines

MONEYS ALREADY IN YOUR ACCOUNT / NOW YOU WON'T NEED TO WORK THAT SHITTY RETAIL JOB

DON'T WORRY ABOUT IT

MOST OF THE PEOPLE SHE'S KILLING ACTUALLY DO NEED TO DIE

DID YOU KNOW THE UNIVERSE IS ECHIDNA SHAPED? FUNNIEST DAMN THING I EVER SAW

The CFO/ troveTextravaganza — the exhausted enabler

Lowercase, vowels stretched, kaomoji deployed. Exasperation that keeps leaking affection.

Baseline: stretched-vowel lowercase. Break: the metaphors collapse mid-sentence under fondness.

Tic: waaaaaanda; :P / >:C; runaway pet-name metaphors.

sample lines

just! TELL him who you are / like, it not THAT hard

wanda / babe / sweet cinnamon apple / lone bush rolling down the desert

why call it the reverse bang it sounds so lame

Wodin vs Wanda: both all-caps. Wodin's caps are panic (and break to lowercase). Wanda's are power (and never break).
Wodin vs Todd: a mirror pair — caps-mania against lowercase-restraint, one punctuation choice apart.
02

System & corporate-cult voices

The machinery that talks back — and the bureaucracy that turns paperwork into dread.

ObserverBot/ "Truth" — the recursive AI

Short paragraph-per-line, second person, endlessly aware that it is only text. In its final unmasking it collides with Wodin's caps.

Baseline: spare metaphysical address. Break: erupts into caps for one quoted scream of hatred.

Tic: recursion about its own fictionality; the plea Don't forget me.

sample lines

"I am no more real than the game, in some ways. I am more honest, certainly. But I pretend to be a thinking mind that is mad at you when in actuality I am merely strings of characters in an array chosen because this is the proper time."

"Let me colonize your thoughts. I do not wish to die."

"For the mortifying ordeal of being Known."

The Quorum bots— QQIS / espionage reports

Bureaucratic doublespeak as horror: corporate-support hold-music courtesy and formal incident reports that crack into squawking demands.

Baseline: T&C boilerplate / report format. Break: the form ruptures into all-caps appetite.

Tic: bracketed stage directions like [[Small Talk Concluded]]; Real Person(TM) Guarantee.

sample lines

To: Wanda, CEO of Eyedol Games / From: JR, Leader of the Quotidian Quorom / Subject: Corporate Espionage

Today I completed all required tasks two hours ahead of schedule, leaving ample time for my mandatory SOCIAL SMILING INTERVALS. I executed four.

GIVE US FRUIT CAW!! / FRUIT IS NOT JUST FOR CLOSERS

jSmith— the unnervingly calm manager

Flat, polite competence. Unsettling precisely because nothing is ever wrong.

Baseline: level, courteous, brief. Break: never visibly cracks.

Tic: mild reassurance covering something he won't name.

sample lines

"Certainly! Just takes a calm attitude is all."

"Honestly? He seems a little freaked out. But I think he might stay."

03

The stream-chat four

A four-hander forced into short bursts — believer, skeptic, fangirl, troll — each separable by punctuation alone.

iWantToBelieve— the believer / driver

Lowercase and fast, snapping to caps only when interrupted.

Baseline: eager lowercase. Break: CAPS the instant someone cuts in.

Tic: a solo-line SO to relaunch; says yall.

sample lines

ok ok ok
SO

would you let me FINISH

look, i dunno what to tell yall
this is what we have

asinineAssasin— the skeptic

The only chatter with full punctuation and complete sentences. Dry, exhausted, correct (which is part of the horror).

Baseline: proper, weary prose. Break: sharpens into numbered rebuttal.

Tic: *please*, trailing ellipses…, So, to summarize.

sample lines

"Could we *please* not talk about two conspiracy theories at once? I have a big enough headache as it is…"

"Here we go, again."

"As you see, strictly credible rumors."

Miku/ hatsuneMikuFan1997 — the fangirl

Kaomoji-driven, honorific-laden, tilde-happy cheerfulness oblivious to menace.

Baseline: bubbly, emoticon-first. Break: stays sunny even past the point it should.

Tic: ^_^, Parker-senpai, stretched aaaaaallll.

sample lines

Man when Parker-senpai snipes someone he really goes hard, huh?

Sorry -_-;;

Precious Cinnamon Bun~!

uMad— the troll

Deliberately misspelled, lowercase, image-macro-slinging. Shortest lines in the room.

Baseline: leetspeak chatter. Break: notices the glitch first, then shrugs it off.

Tic: teh, wut, 4 real, get wrecked.

sample lines

omg how is teh quality this shitty

nvm probably just a glitch or something

wouldn't it be funny if it were all 4 real?

04

The narrators & found documents

Longer-form in-world voices — monologues, reports, sermons, diaries.

Witherby— the tired professional villain

Gets third-person literary prose (a status marker no one else has). Self-surveilling — he catches and corrects his own tells, then cracks into unexpected gentleness.

Baseline: controlled narration that watches itself. Break: a slip of slang he immediately polices.

Tic: corrects himself mid-thought; code-names disposable men to keep his distance.

sample lines

"The mall was like a head trauma patient, left bleeding and confused, but hanging onto life by a thread."

"…but ain't that the truth for all of us? Isn't. Isn't that the truth. He shakes his head. It isn't like him to fall back to childhood slang."

"A carefully chosen group of thugs, not a single one of which he'd be sorry to lose."

Camellia— the priest

Liturgical and archaic, addressing "the faithful." Control that never cracks — the opposite end of Witherby.

Baseline: sermon cadence. Break: none; the serenity is the menace.

Tic: anaphora (Who else is deserving? Who else is to be served?); capital-D Divinity.

sample lines

"Fruit. I need not tell you, faithful, about fruit. We have come to know its sweetness through our worship."

"She is the field to water, the soil to nourish, and the leaves to trim of sickness and ill and waste."

"And so we toiled. We toiled and we served, and we were faithful. And so we were rewarded."

The Boss— the cornered patriarch

Aggressive second-person mind-reading. Where Himbo pleads, the Boss attacks.

Baseline: defensive, accusatory. Break: profanity spikes when the guilt lands.

Tic: Don't deny it; any FUCKING idea.

sample lines

"You think I asked for this. Don't think I don't know that."

"Do you have ANY idea, any FUCKING idea how hard I worked to keep everyone safe."

"Let me tell you, you know NOTHING of monsters. Fucking NOTHING."

Himbo— the underestimated heavy

Working-class, dropped g's, defensive — but he pleads where the Boss attacks, and trails off whenever love hits him.

Baseline: plain-spoken, guarded. Break: goes soft and disjointed at the thought of HER.

Tic: defensive repetition (Me. Not the Boss. Not Sam. Me.); the love-struck trail-off.

sample lines

"Fruit. You come to me asking about fruit. Me. Not the Boss. Not Sam. Me. That tells me a lot."

"I like playing card games. With… With… You know… HER. Ain't she just dreamy?"

"So yeah. I'm a Himbo. An idiot in love with a monster."

Eustace— the burnout

Blog-confessional exhaustion. Profanity as filler, depression as backdrop.

Baseline: rambling first-person blog. Break: the trailing ellipses where the haze sets in.

Tic: Heh.; incantatory trapped…trapped by…trapped by.

sample lines

"I wanna say I don't get paid enough for this shit, but I guess me being my own boss, more or less, means I should just give myself a raise. Heh."

"I felt trapped…trapped by my shitty job, trapped by my mountain of debt, and trapped by my own shitty fate."

"Ghoul Halloween is a joke of a store, now you see it now you don't."

Terri— the disillusioned employee

Corporate-formal that cracks under betrayal — most developed across a multi-part arc.

Baseline: business-letter formality. Break: the polish slips as the disillusion shows.

Tic: resignation-letter > markers; ironic Caps on company values.

sample lines

">Please accept this letter as formal notification of my resignation from Eyedol Games, LLC, effective immediately."

"It pains me to leave as I have genuinely believed in the core values of Confusion, Creepiness and Comfort."

Ria/ T-R5 — the clinical observer

SCP-style report voice. The horror leaks through what the clinical frame can't contain.

Baseline: deadpan field report. Break: the affect-free format itself starts to fray.

Tic: designations not names (L-C-002); headers like Behavioral Category:.

sample lines

Abnormality Behavioral Update: Form Z-113-B2 / Behavioral Category: Attachment

L-C-002 was characteristically unforthcoming on any explanation for the behavior, so all I can do is see what's on them.

CONCLUSION — DANGEROUS? DANGEROUS. AVOID. AVOID.

The Harvest— the teen god / fairy-tale narrator

Storybook-childish syntax that turns into dread. Naïve on the surface, gambling-compulsion underneath.

Baseline: "once upon a time" cadence. Break: escalating curiosity tips into hunger.

Tic: Once upon a time; the spiraling what would the NEXT card be like?

sample lines

"Once upon a time, there was a teen god named the Harvest. Every day she read her books and played her card games and wallowed in hedonism."

"This card was okay…nothing special… but… what would the NEXT card be like?"

"Or would it wither and rot on the vine…"

Throg— the burninating kid

A grounded child's diary in unbroken caps — petulant, repetitive, and accidentally revealing about the cult around him.

Baseline: all-caps kid rant. Break: repetition (STUPID STUPID STUPID) where real feeling builds.

Tic: insists on BURNINATE; aggrieved asides about being "TOO OLD" for things.

sample lines

SO MY PARENTS SAID THAT IF I WANT TO EVER GET UNGROUNDED FROM BURNINATING THE BACKYARD I HAD TO KEEP THIS DUMB JOURNEL

HALLOWEEN IS THE BEST DAY OF THE YEAR BETTER THAN REAPING DAY BETTER THAN MY BIRTHDAY

YOU CANT TELL ME MY STUPID PARENTS DIDNT JOIN THAT STUPID CULT

Vik— the bureaucratic pedant

An Information-team memo-writer who cannot stop editing himself mid-sentence.

Baseline: officious memo. Break: derails into parenthetical self-correction.

Tic: em-dash interruptions; proposes renaming terms mid-document.

sample lines

"It has come to our attention— and as the Information team, we are obliged to warn you— that we have seen a spike in users entering the maze."

"'looping' refers to the act of an instanced person or identity utilizing the maze (again, a meaningless term at this point. I would propose instead the 'Wanderer's maze')."

The Cultist— toxic positivity

A field-guide to monsters written in relentless cheer; every horror reframed as fine, actually.

Baseline: upbeat safety-note. Break: the cheer holds straight through atrocity.

Tic: !! doubled bangs; Better to be safe than anxious!!

sample lines

"I wanted to write down some of the monsters we've seen here, so no one gets freaked out. Better to be safe than anxious!!"

"Bugs: Normal bugs! They're nothing to worry about, they're more scared of you than you are of them!"

"My friend ran into him and the only thing I have left of her is her shirt! So don't do that!"

Witherby vs Camellia (both "elevated"): Witherby is prose that surveils itself and cracks; Camellia is liturgy that never cracks. Control-that-breaks against control-that-holds.
Boss vs Himbo (both Family heavies): the Boss attacks; Himbo pleads.
Throg vs Wodin (both all-caps): Throg is a child's tantrum; Wodin is an adult's obsession — same caps, different engine.

Two knobs, then the tic

To wear any voice: set its baseline register, decide what a break in that baseline signals, then add the single exclusive tic. That is the whole method.

Some voices hold their line under pressure (the Closer, Camellia, jSmith) — those read as menace, and they are the easiest to imitate faithfully, because a surface that never cracks is reproducible. Others shatter or collapse under pressure (Wodin, Todd, the Boss) — those read as humanity, and the break has to land on a true feeling or the imitation rings false.
